# MNB Research

Indian AI company building tools for finance, audit, tax and legal teams, and AbroBot.ai for students going abroad. Founded by Mridul Nanda, Chartered Accountant. DPIIT recognised, featured on Shark Tank India.

Website: https://mnbresearch.com

## Products

**AuditFlow** (https://auditflow.mnbresearch.com): offline mobile audits with GPS and device-stamped photo proof; every failed checkpoint becomes an action with an owner, SLA and countdown; IFC/CARO mapping; 344 templates across 36 frameworks plus unlimited custom AI audit campaigns; tamper-evident trail and one-click evidence pack.

**NyayaAI** (https://nyaya.mnbresearch.com): drafts legal and tax notice replies cited to the exact section and case; 250+ legal tools; limitation dates; IPC to BNS mapping. Free plan with 5 credits a month.

**MNB Cortex** (https://cortex.mnbresearch.com): reads Tally, Zoho Books, Vyapar, Busy and Marg exports; flags overdue receivables and the 43B(h) MSME 45-day clock; tracks GST, TDS, advance tax, ROC and audit dates; 13-week cash flow and a Monday brief.

**AbroBot.ai** (https://abrobot.ai): AI study-abroad mentor; matches universities across 24,080 verified institutions, SOP analysis with an originality check, scholarships, visa guidance and deadline reminders. Free, 5 credits a month.

## Open source

**mnb-claude-skills**: skills for Claude and AI agents (Indian legal docs, GST, startup compliance and more). **india-equity-research-skill**: equity research for Indian listed companies. **nimbus-local**: a fast local AWS emulator.
